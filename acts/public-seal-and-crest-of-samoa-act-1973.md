# Public Seal and Crest of Samoa Act 1973

AN ACT to provide for the form and use of the Public Seal, and of the Crest of Samoa and for related purposes.

[Assent and commencement date: 11 January 1974]

#### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Seal and Crest of Samoa Act 1973.

#### 2. Interpretation

In this Act:

“Public Seal” means the Public Seal of Samoa referred to in Articles 29 and 111 of the Constitution.

#### 3. Form of the Public Seal

The Public Seal shall be in the form described in the Schedule to this Act.

#### 4. Custody of the Public Seal

The Public Seal shall be in the custody of the Head of State.

#### 5. Use of the Public Seal

The Public Seal may be used for the authentication of a public document in relation to the Government of Samoa, or for the execution of a document required by law to be executed under the Seal of Samoa.

#### 6. Other Seals

The Head of State, acting on the advice of Cabinet, may approve other seals as official seals of Samoa, and may designate the manner of their use.

#### 7. Crest of Samoa

The official crest or badge of Samoa shall be in the same form as the form of the Public Seal, excluding therefrom the surrounding concentric circles, and the words “The Public Seal of the Independent State of Samoa”.

#### 8. Use of the Crest of Samoa

The Crest of Samoa may be printed or impressed on any official letter, memorandum, report, document or other paper writing issued by the Government of Samoa.

#### 9. Repeal

Section 44 of the Samoa Act 1921 (New Zealand) is repealed as part of the law of Samoa.
